# Offline Guardian - Privacy Notice

## What data we collect
- **Location:** Exact GPS location to dispatch help during an SOS event.
- **Media (Camera/Mic):** Audio and photos captured automatically during an SOS event for evidence.
- **Device Info:** A generic Unique ID (UUID) for abuse prevention. We DO NOT collect your IMEI.
- **Contacts:** (Optional) Trusted contacts to alert.

## Why we collect it
- Data is solely used to coordinate emergency rescue with ERSS-112 and trusted contacts.
- Background location is used during an active SOS to continuously update responders.
- Bluetooth/Mesh data is used strictly for relaying SOS signals when offline.

## Retention and Deletion
- Incident records and media are automatically deleted after 90 days unless requested by law enforcement.
- You can withdraw consent and delete your data at any time via the App Settings > "Delete My Data". This will permanently purge your records from our servers.
